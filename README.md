# Glory Player
Intuitive Android Audio Player and Voice Recorder


An offline, privacy-focused folder-tree audio player and voice recorder for Android. Built for people who keep their own local audio files and want zero tracking, zero ads, and zero cloud accounts.

* **Multi-Folder Tree Stacking:** Open multiple folders side-by-side and browse nested subfolders without flattening your directory structure.
* **Play Folder / Play All Toggle:** Play a single folder or seamlessly advance across loaded folders and subfolders.
* **Built-In Voice Recorder:** Record voice memos straight to local storage.
* **Timestamp Bookmarks:** Save and jump back to exact playback positions per track.
* **100% Offline & Private:** No internet permissions required for playback, no Google Play dependencies, and no telemetry.

## How to Install on Android

1. Download **app-release.apk** on your Android device.
2. Open the downloaded file from your browser's Downloads or file manager.
3. When prompted by Android, allow **"Install unknown apps"** for that browser or file manager, then tap **Install**.
